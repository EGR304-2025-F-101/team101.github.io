## Project Objective
This project aims to design and develop a **Water-Efficient Lawn Watering System** that helps homeowners, renters, and environmentally conscious individuals maintain healthy lawns and landscapes while minimizing water usage and costs. The system will leverage adaptive irrigation technologies, smart scheduling, and intuitive interfaces to make lawn care effortless and sustainable.  

Our goal is to outperform existing competitive products by offering:
- **Higher water efficiency** (reduced consumption, optimized schedules)
- **Greater reliability** (robust outdoor-ready design, fewer connectivity issues)
- **Improved accessibility** (simple setup, easy-to-use mobile app)

By integrating these features, we aim to reduce water waste, lower household utility bills, and contribute to environmentally responsible lawn care.

## Stakeholders
- **Primary Users**: Homeowners and renters who want lush, healthy lawns while conserving resources  
- **Target Purchasers**: Families, particularly those managing household expenses, who value cost savings and convenience  
- **Customer Service**: Seeks intuitive systems that are easy to troubleshoot, repair, and maintain  
- **Marketing & Sales Division**: Focuses on highlighting water savings, sustainability, and smart lifestyle integration  
- **Retailers**: Require durable, compact products that perform reliably under diverse climate and storage conditions  

## Use Cases

### User Story #1: Daniel
Daniel is a busy father of two who wants his lawn looking fresh but has little time for manual watering. His new system automatically adjusts to weather forecasts and local restrictions, saving him time and lowering his water bill. He can check and adjust watering schedules directly from his phone, even while at work.

### User Story #2: Maria
Maria is a renter who wants a greener lawn without dealing with complex installations. She installs the controller in under 20 minutes, connects it to WiFi, and lets the system’s soil moisture sensors do the work. She appreciates that it prevents overwatering during heavy rain, keeping her landlord happy while conserving resources.

## Aspects

### Producer Design
- **1.1** The product shall be visually distinct from major competitors (Rachio, Netro, Rain Bird, Hunter, Scotts GRO) while maintaining a clean, modern aesthetic. (P10)  
- **1.2** The product shall reflect environmentally friendly values (compact, recyclable, outdoor-ready). (P8)  
- **1.3** The design shall minimize unnecessary parts/components for easier installation. (P7)  

### Functionality
- **2.1** The system shall include an intuitive mobile app for setup, scheduling, and remote control. (P10)  
- **2.2** The device shall support adaptive scheduling using weather data and soil moisture levels. (P10)  
- **2.3** The system shall integrate with major smart home platforms (Alexa, Google Home). (P7)  
- **2.4** The controller shall operate reliably under outdoor conditions (temperature, rain, humidity). (P9)  
- **2.5** The system shall continue functioning during WiFi interruptions and recover automatically after outages. (P9)  

## Requirement Criteria Specifications
- Regulate system power for both mains and solar inputs (9–12 V)  
- Provide accurate zone-based scheduling with up to 12 programmable zones  
- Offer app-based water usage reports with accuracy ±5%  
- Ensure waterproof casing rated at least IPX4  
- Minimize installation time to less than 30 minutes with standard tools  

## Categories & Main Needs
- **Efficiency**: Device conserves water and adapts to environmental conditions  
- **Convenience & Usability**: Simple setup, intuitive app, minimal learning curve  
- **Durability & Reliability**: Outdoor-ready, backup power, resistant to outages  
- **Cost & Sustainability**: Affordable (< $200), reduces water bills, supports renewable energy  
- **Smart Features**: App control, reporting, smart home integration  

## Prioritization of Needs
- **High Priority (5)**: Water efficiency, reliable automatic operation, intuitive app, durability, simple installation  
- **Medium Priority (3)**: Smart home integration, affordable cost, flexible zone control  
- **Low Priority (1)**: Aesthetic appeal, optional accessories/expansions  

## Open Questions
- Can solar charging be made a default feature for energy independence?  
- What is the optimal balance between affordability and advanced features (e.g., sensors, cloud integration)?  
- How can we design for modular upgrades while keeping the base product cost-effective?
