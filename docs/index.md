## Team Introduction
Welcome to Team 101’s Water-Efficient Lawn Watering System.

We are a small group of engineering students working together to design and build a smart watering system that saves water while keeping lawns healthy. Our goal is to make something simple, reliable, and easy for anyone to use, whether you’re a homeowner, renter, or someone who just wants to handle watering needs without wasting water.

We started this project to learn through hands-on engineering experience, grow our skills, and build something we’re proud to share. Each of us focuses on a part of the system like controls, sensors, motors, and we bring it all together into one working design.

If you’d like to see more about our work, please check out the tabs above!
